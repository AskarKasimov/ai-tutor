[D21 | Week 6 Lab 5 (without solution).pdf | с.8 | core]
Exploration vs Exploitation
If we only focus on exploitation, our agent will never reach the gigantic sum
of cheese. Instead, it will only exploit the nearest source of rewards, even if
this source is small (exploitation).
But if our agent does a little bit of exploration, it can discover the big
reward (the pile of big cheese).
This is what we call the exploration/exploitation trade-off. We need to
balance how much we explore the environment and how much we exploit
what we know about the environment.
